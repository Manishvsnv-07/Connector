# Connector 🔗

> A decentralized social web app where creators and users connect directly — no middleman, no platform cuts.

## What is Connector?

Connector is a social media platform built on top of Solana blockchain where creators can share content and earn directly from their audience through **SOL tipping** and **NFT minting** — without any middleman like Instagram or YouTube taking a cut.

Users can support their favorite creators by sending SOL tips directly via Phantom Wallet. Every post can be minted as an NFT, giving creators another way to earn from their content.

## Features

- 📝 **Post Content** — Share images, videos, and text with your audience
- ❤️ **Likes & Comments** — Engage with creators and their content
- 💸 **SOL Tipping** — Send SOL directly to creators via Phantom Wallet, peer-to-peer, no middleman
- 🖼️ **NFT Minting** — Every post can be minted as an NFT so creators can sell their content
- 🔐 **Phantom Wallet Login** — Connect your Solana wallet to authenticate and transact
- 👥 **Follow System** — Follow creators and build your audience
- 🎥 **Video & Image Uploads** — Rich media support via Cloudinary

## How It Works

1. User signs up and connects their **Phantom Wallet**
2. Creators post content — images, videos, or text
3. Each post can be **minted as an NFT** on Solana
4. Viewers can **tip SOL directly** to the creator with one click
5. No platform fees, no middleman — 100% goes to the creator

## Why Connector?

Platforms like Instagram and YouTube take huge cuts from creator earnings and control monetization. Connector removes that barrier — creators own their content as NFTs and receive tips directly on-chain via Solana, making it fast and nearly free.
